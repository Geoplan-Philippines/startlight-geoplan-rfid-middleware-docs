Executive Summary

Provide a high-level overview of the project, including the business opportunity, objectives, and expected benefits.

The company intends to implement Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology across its
retail ecosystem to improve inventory accuracy, operational efficiency, and visibility across
warehouses and stores. This initiative will integrate RFID hardware and middleware with the
existing SAP ERP landscape, enabling automated, near­real-time inventory tracking and
event-driven business processes.

The implementation will cover inbound, outbound, internal movement, sales, and return
scenarios across warehouses, stores, and selected office locations. RFID events will be logically
aggregated and translated into meaningful business events before being posted to SAP,
ensuring scalability, performance, and data accuracy.

Expected benefits include:

     Improved inventory accuracy and visibility across warehouses and stores
     Reduced labor effort and cycle count time
     Faster inventory reconciliation and exception detection
     Enhanced customer experience via accurate product availability
     Stronger readiness/support for omnichannel fulfillment

Business Objective

List the business goals this project aims to fulfill.

    1. Achieve inventory accuracy across warehouses and stores.
    2. Reduce manual scanning and counting effort in stores and warehouses
    3. Enable near­real-time visibility of inventory movement
    4. Improve store shelf availability and replenishment responsiveness
    5. Support faster and more reliable store receiving and sales return processing
    6. Provide a scalable RFID foundation that supports future rollout to additional stores and

         warehouses

Background and Current State

Describe the current business process and the problem or opportunity prompting the project.

Current State

Currently, inventory movements are tracked primarily through barcode scanning and manual
processes within SAP. Store cycle counts and stock validation are labor-intensive,
time-consuming, and prone to human error. Inventory discrepancies are often discovered late,
impacting replenishment, availability, and customer satisfaction.
Inbound and outbound processes rely heavily on manual confirmation steps. Store operations,
including receiving, shelf replenishment, and returns, lack automated validation mechanisms.
Visibility into shelf-level availability versus backroom stock is limited.

Opportunity

RFID technology provides an opportunity to automate inventory capture, reduce manual
intervention, and enable continuous inventory awareness. Observations from mature RFID retail
implementations (e.g., fashion retailers) indicate significant improvements in cycle count speed,
store accuracy, and operational efficiency when RFID is embedded into daily store workflows.

Scope of Work

Identify what is within and out of scope of the solution coverage.

In Scope

     RFID Events Management
               Capture RFID tag reads from fixed and handheld readers
               Aggregate raw RFID reads into logical business events using defined time
                   windows and filtering rules
               Support logical grouping of readers into business zones (e.g., warehouse
                   inbound dock, warehouse outbound dock, store backroom, sales floor, POS)
               Generate standardized RFID business events (e.g., Goods Receipt, Goods
                   Issue, Stock Transfer, Sale, Return)

     Logical Zoning & Reader Grouping
               Support logical grouping of RFID readers into business zones such as:
                         Warehouse inbound docks
                         Warehouse outbound docks
                         Staging / buffer zones
                         Store backroom receiving
                         Sales floor, POS checkout, and exit zones
                         Apply business rules at the zone level, not per individual reader
                         Prevent cross-zone misreads (e.g., inbound reads interpreted as
                             outbound)

     Warehouse Operations:
               Inbound: Supplier deliveries (local & foreign), warehouse transfers, returns to
                   vendor, returns from sales
               Outbound: Shipments to stores, sales fulfillment, transfers to head office

     Store Operations:
               Store receiving via backroom RFID zones
               Separation of backroom inventory and sales-floor availability
               RFID-enabled cycle counting (full and partial)
               Shelf replenishment support based on inventory visibility
               Sales and checkout inventory decrement (RFID-assisted or validated)
               Customer sales returns processing
     RFID hardware deployment (tags, fixed/mobile readers)
     SAP Integration
               Integration of aggregated RFID business events into SAP (e.g., MM, SD, EWM,

                   SAP Retail)
               Posting of inventory movements using bulk and aggregated transactions
               Support for error handling, retries, and reconciliation
               Auditability of RFID-triggered postings
     Redesign affected processes:
               Tagging of RFID Tags
               Receiving and put-away
               Picking, packing, shipping
               Stock replenishment, cycle counting, returns
     Reporting and Monitoring
               Operational dashboards for RFID events and exceptions
               Visibility into inventory accuracy, cycle count performance, and exception rates.
     Exception Handling & Governance
               Classification of RFID exceptions (missing reads, extra reads, unexpected exits,

                   duplicate detections)
               Role-based manual override capabilities
               Full audit trail for overrides and adjustments
               Clear ownership of exception resolution (store vs warehouse vs HQ)
     User training and change management

Out of Scope

     Core SAP ERP redesign
     Non-inventory use cases (marketing/customer tracking)
     RFID for non-retail business units or external partners
     Full replacement of barcodes where still required
     Advanced analytics beyond basic inventory updates
