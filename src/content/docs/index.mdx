Geoplan middleware turns reader hardware into EPC reads your system can consume. Define the business processes you scan under ([scan activities](/scan-activities/)), then open a [scan session](/epc-scan-processing/) for each read — the reader turns on, streams EPCs into the session, you read the deduped result, you stop. ETP POS and Samooha integrate through the same two modules.

## Integrate in three steps

1. Get an API key — [Authentication](/authentication/).
2. Create the [scan activities](/scan-activities/) for your processes (POS Sale, Goods Receipt, …). Keep their `id`s.
3. Per scan: `POST` a [session](/epc-scan-processing/) with an activity + reader, poll it, `complete` it, read the `epcs`.
