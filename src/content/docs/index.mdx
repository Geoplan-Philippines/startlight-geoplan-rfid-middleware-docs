## How it works

ETP POS and Samooha can use different endpoints, fields, response structures, pagination, authentication, and change-tracking conventions. Geoplan reads each source and maps it to the middleware's internal model; providers never call Geoplan for master-data sync.

<Steps>

1. **The provider exposes its existing product master.**

   The source remains authoritative and keeps its native structure.

2. **Geoplan retrieves and maps the source.**

   A provider-specific adapter handles access, pagination, change tracking, and mapping.

3. **The RFID layer adds EPC/TID associations.**

   These associations remain in Geoplan and do not change the provider's product model.

</Steps>
